> Placeholders: `[PRODUCT]` (product name), `[DOMAIN]`, `[ADDRESS]`, `[ENTITY]` (legal form once registered).

**[PRODUCT]** is run by **Nidanos** ("we", "us"). Until Nidanos is incorporated, its founders run it together, and after that the company `[ENTITY]` takes over. This notice explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and your rights under India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** (DPDP Act) and the DPDP Rules, 2025.

You can read this notice on its own; it doesn't depend on any other document.

| | |

## 2. The data we collect, and why
| Data | Examples | Why we need it |
|---|---|---|
| **Account** | Email, display name, password (stored only as a one-way hash), date of birth, time zone | To create your account, sign you in, check whether you need parental consent, and show times correctly |
| **Sign-in security** | Device type (browser or app), IP address, sign-in and password-reset times | To keep your account safe and detect stolen sessions |
| **Learning activity** | Courses you join, the code you submit, test results, hints used, lesson notes written for you, time spent, quiz answers, your self-ratings | To grade your work, measure what you know, and choose your next lesson |
| **Progress** | Mastery scores per skill, review schedule | To adapt lessons and remind you to review before you forget |
| **Messages to us** | Support or grievance emails | To answer you |

We collect only what we need for these purposes. **We do not collect** your phone number, contacts, location, photos, payment card numbers (a payment provider handles those once paid plans start), or any government ID (except as section 7 needs for parental verification).

**Your code:** we keep the code you write only until you master that skill. After that we delete it and keep only the result (for example, "passed, 1 hint").

## 3. Consent
When you sign up, you agree to this notice so that we can use your data for the purposes in section 2. Without that we can't provide [PRODUCT].

Two further uses are **optional**, **off by default**, and have their own tick boxes:

| Optional use | What it means | Who can opt in |
|---|---|---|
| **AI model training** | We use de-identified learning data to train or improve our own AI models | Adults only |
| **Research** | We use de-identified data in Nidanos's academic study and in published research papers | Adults only |

"De-identified" means we remove your name, email and any other details that point to you.

You can **withdraw any consent at any time**, as easily as you gave it: **Settings → Privacy**, or email `privacy@[DOMAIN]`. Withdrawing doesn't affect what we did before you withdrew. Withdrawing your main consent means closing your account (section 9).

## 4. Improving [PRODUCT]
We study **de-identified and aggregated** data, such as "40% of learners needed a hint on this exercise", to fix confusing exercises and improve hints. This never uses anything that identifies you.

## 5. Who we share data with
We **never sell** personal data. We share it only with service providers (data processors) who work for us under written contracts. They may use it only to run [PRODUCT], must keep it secure, and must delete it when asked.

| Provider type | What they get | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Database hosting (India region) | All the data in section 2 | Storing it |
| Application hosting (Singapore) | All the data in section 2, while our servers process it | Running our servers |
| Code sandbox | Your submitted code and the exercise's tests, but no name or email | Running your code safely, in isolation |
| AI model provider (on paid, no-training terms) | Your code and the exercise context; for lesson notes, your scores and recent results, not your code. Never your name or email | Writing hints, explanations and lesson notes |
| Email delivery | Your email address | Sign-in and account emails |
| Payment provider (once paid plans start) | What the payment needs | Taking payments |

The current list of named providers is on our [providers page](/privacy/providers). We may also disclose data when Indian law or a court order requires it.

## 6. Where your data is stored
Our database is hosted **in India**. Our application servers run in **Singapore**, and some other providers in section 5, like the code sandbox, the AI model provider and the email delivery service, may also process data **outside India**. The DPDP Act allows this, except to countries the Government of India restricts, and we never send data to those countries. We send only what each task needs: the code sandbox and AI model provider never get your name or email, and the email delivery service gets only your email address.

## 7. Children (under 18)
Anyone can learn on [PRODUCT], but **if you are under 18, a parent or legal guardian must approve your account first**. Until then we don't process your learning data.

**During the pilot, accounts are for adults (18 and over) only.** The rest of this section applies once parent approval is ready.

- At sign-up you enter your date of birth. If you're under 18, we email your parent or guardian, who approves from their own adult account. We'll add stronger identity checks before May 2027, as the DPDP Rules require.
- For children we **never** show ads, use their data for **AI training or research**, or track them for anything except teaching them what their parent approved.
- A parent or guardian can see, correct or delete their child's data, or withdraw consent, at any time.

## 9. How long we keep data
| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Account and progress | Until you delete your account. If you're **inactive for 3 years**, we email you **48 hours** before deleting it, and signing in stops the deletion. |
| Your code | Until you master that skill, then deleted |
| Sign-in security records | At least 1 year, as the law requires, for investigating security incidents |
| Consent records | As long as we need to prove you gave consent, and no longer than 3 years after account deletion |
| Payment records (once paid plans start) | As tax law requires (up to 8 years) |
| De-identified data (sections 3–4) | It can no longer identify you, so it may be kept |

**When you delete your account**, we wait **7 days** in case you change your mind: signing in during that time cancels the deletion. Then we erase your name, email, password, date of birth and code. Your progress is deleted too, unless you opted in to AI training or research: then only de-identified numbers (such as test results and scores) stay. Records we must keep, such as proof of consent, stay linked only to a random ID.

## 11. Security
Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, sign-in tokens are short-lived, all connections use HTTPS, and your code runs in an isolated sandbox. If a breach affects you, we'll tell you without delay: what happened, the likely impact, what we're doing about it, and what you can do.

## 12. Cookies
We use only **two essential cookies**, both needed to keep you signed in: `at` (expires after 15 minutes) and `rt` (30 days). We use **no** analytics, advertising or third-party cookies.

## 13. Changes to this notice
If we make an important change, we'll tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect. If the change needs new consent, we'll ask for it.
