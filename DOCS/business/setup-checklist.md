# Business setup checklist

> In order. Each step unblocks the next. Confirm fees and forms with a **Chartered Accountant (CA)** before filing, because government rules change.
> ✅ = done · ⏳ = in progress · ⬜ = to do
> Costs are 2026 estimates for **Delhi** (3 directors, ₹1 lakh authorised capital). Sources disagree slightly, especially on Delhi stamp duty; confirm on the MCA portal and the Delhi e-stamp site.

## Phase A: now (almost free, protects you immediately)
| # | Step | Why | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Sign the founders' agreement** ([founders-agreement.md](founders-agreement.md)) on Delhi e-stamp paper (~₹50–100), with 2 witnesses; notarisation optional (~₹200–500) | Settles ownership, vesting and IP before money or conflict exists. Until the company is registered, this is what binds the three founders | ⬜ |
| A2 | **College NOC** from JIMS ([college-noc-letter.md](college-noc-letter.md)) | Removes any later claim on the IP | ⬜ |
| A3 | **Pick the product name.** Check it's free in the **IP India public trademark search**, as a domain, and as social handles | A generic name can't be protected; check before you build a brand | ⬜ |
| A4 | Domain + business email (**nidanos.com**). Still to add: `support@`, `privacy@`, `grievance@` and `security@` mailboxes | Needed by the Privacy Policy, Terms and breach plan | ⏳ |
| A5 | Repo hygiene: private repo, only third-party content whose licence allows commercial use, history cleaned | Clean IP for buyers and investors | ✅ |
| A6 | **LinkedIn company page** linked to all three founders | Startup programs and incubators look for it | ✅ |
| A7 | **Landing page** at nidanos.com. Today it presents a freelance agency; change it to show the **AI Tutor product** (problem, how it works, team, contact) | Startup programs check that you're a product startup | ⏳ |
| A8 | **One-page pitch**: problem, solution, traction (pilot numbers), team, ask | Needed for incubators and credit programs; the Phase 3 report has the content | ⬜ |

## Phase B: before the first paying customer

**Timing: register after the MVP and pilot**, right before you need the company (startup credits, taking payments, joining an incubator). From the day it's registered, a Pvt Ltd company owes a **yearly audit and filings even with zero revenue**, about ₹15,000–35,000 a year. Ask the JIMS incubation cell first: incubators often help with incorporation for free or at a discount.

| # | Step | Notes | Cost | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Incorporate a Private Limited Company** on the MCA portal (SPICe+ form) | Needs at least 2 directors and 2 shareholders (you have 3). Each director needs a **DSC** (digital signature) and a **DIN**. Name approval happens in the same form. PAN and TAN come with the certificate. A CA usually handles it end to end | DSC ₹3,000–7,500 (3 × 2-year, with token) · name ₹0–1,000 · MCA fee ₹0 (capital ≤ ₹15 lakh) · Delhi stamp duty on MoA + AoA ~₹400–2,000 · office proof ₹0–200 · CA/CS fee ₹5,000–15,000. **Total ~₹9,000–25,000** | ⬜ |
| B2 | Sign the **Shareholders' Agreement** + **IP assignment deed** to the company | Moves the founders' agreement and all pre-incorporation work into the company | Lawyer ₹10,000–25,000 | ⬜ |
| B3 | Post-incorporation basics | Open a **current bank account** in the company's name and deposit the share capital (e.g. ₹10,000 per founder; it stays company money). Appoint an auditor (**ADT-1, within 30 days**). File the commencement declaration (**INC-20A, within 180 days**; ⚠️ missing it costs a **₹50,000 penalty**) | Bank ₹0 with a zero-balance startup account (some banks lock ₹10–25k as minimum balance) · ADT-1 ~₹1,500–3,500 · INC-20A ~₹1,000–2,000 | ⬜ |
| B4 | **Udyam (MSME) registration** | Online. Gives lower trademark fees and MSME benefits | ₹0 | ⬜ |
| B5 | **DPIIT Startup India recognition** | Cheaper IP filings, self-certification for some labour and environmental laws, eligibility for tax benefits, stronger startup-program applications | ₹0 | ⬜ |
| B6 | **File the trademark** in classes **9** (software), **41** (education) and **42** (SaaS) | The startup/MSME fee is lower per class. File as soon as the name is final | ₹4,500 per class × 3 + attorney ≈ ₹17,000–20,000 | ⬜ |
| B7 | **Publish the legal pages** at `nidanos.com/privacy`, `/terms`, `/refunds` and `/contact` | Payment gateways require these pages before approving you | ₹0 | ⬜ |
| B8 | **Payment gateway** (e.g. Razorpay) | Needs the company PAN, bank account, website and the legal pages | No setup fee; ~2% per payment | ⬜ |
| B9 | **Lawyer review** of the Privacy Policy, Terms, parental flow and Shareholders' Agreement | One review with good drafts is quick | Included in B2 if it's the same lawyer | ⬜ |

## Phase B+: startup credit programs (after B1 and B5)
Apply right before the pilot or launch, because the credits expire in 1–2 years. The no-investor tiers are small (about $1,000–5,000 each); bigger tiers need an incubator, accelerator or VC referral.

| # | Program | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Microsoft for Startups** | Sign up, then business verification for the extra credits | ⬜ |
| P2 | **Google for Startups Cloud** (Start tier; credits usable for Gemini via Vertex AI) | Company details + product website; apply from the company's own Google Cloud account | ⬜ |
| P3 | **AWS Activate Founders** | Company AWS account **upgraded to the paid plan first**; business email on the domain; working website | ⬜ |
| P4 | **Join an incubator** (start with the JIMS incubation cell) | Unlocks referrals and the AWS Org ID for the $25k–100k+ tiers | ⬜ |
| P5 | **Ask E2B** for startup or student credits | None of the cloud programs covers sandbox costs | ⬜ |

## Phase C: as you grow
| # | Trigger | Step |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Turnover over **₹20 lakh a year** (services) | **GST registration** becomes mandatory; you can register voluntarily earlier. Charge 18% GST on subscriptions once registered |
| C2 | Selling to colleges | A standard **institution agreement**: seats, price, data roles (DPDP fiduciary vs processor), SLA |
| C3 | First employee or intern | Offer letter with an **IP assignment** and confidentiality clause; register under the Delhi Shops & Establishments Act |
| C4 | First outside investor | Term sheet, valuation, and an ESOP pool (unanimous decision, founders' agreement clause 6) |
| C5 | Real user base | **Cyber liability insurance**, and a yearly security review |

## Every year (Private Limited Company)
- **Statutory audit** of accounts (mandatory, even with zero revenue)
- **ROC filings:** financial statements (AOC-4) and annual return (MGT-7/7A)
- **Income tax return** (ITR-6), plus directors' KYC
- **GST returns**, monthly or quarterly once registered

Budget a CA retainer of **about ₹15,000–35,000 a year** for these, plus small government filing fees. Missing ROC filings means daily late fees.
