# Business setup checklist

> In order. Each step unblocks the next. Confirm fees and forms with a **Chartered Accountant (CA)** before filing, because government rules change.
> ✅ = done · ⏳ = in progress · ⬜ = to do

## Phase A: now (free, protects you immediately)
| # | Step | Why | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Sign the founders' agreement** ([founders-agreement.md](founders-agreement.md)) on e-stamp paper, with 2 witnesses | Settles ownership, vesting and IP before money or conflict exists | ⬜ |
| A2 | **College NOC** from JIMS ([college-noc-letter.md](college-noc-letter.md)) | Removes any later claim on the IP | ⬜ |
| A3 | **Pick the product name.** Check it's free in the **IP India public trademark search**, as a domain, and as social handles | A generic name can't be protected; check before you build a brand | ⬜ |
| A4 | Buy the **domain**. Set up `support@`, `privacy@`, `grievance@` and `security@` mailboxes | Needed by the Privacy Policy, Terms and breach plan | ⬜ |
| A5 | Repo hygiene: private repo, only third-party content whose licence allows commercial use, history cleaned | Clean IP for buyers and investors | ✅ |

## Phase B: before the first paying customer
| # | Step | Notes | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Incorporate a Private Limited Company** on the MCA portal (SPICe+ form) | Needs at least 2 directors and 2 shareholders (you have 3). Each director needs a **DSC** (digital signature) and a **DIN**. Name approval happens in the same form. PAN and TAN come with the certificate. A CA usually handles it end to end | ⬜ |
| B2 | Sign the **Shareholders' Agreement** + **IP assignment deed** to the company | Moves the founders' agreement and all pre-incorporation work into the company | ⬜ |
| B3 | Post-incorporation basics | Appoint an auditor (within 30 days); file the commencement-of-business declaration (within 180 days); open a **current bank account** in the company's name | ⬜ |
| B4 | **Udyam (MSME) registration** | Free, online. Gives lower trademark fees and MSME benefits | ⬜ |
| B5 | **DPIIT Startup India recognition** | Free. Benefits: cheaper IP filings, self-certification for some labour and environmental laws, and eligibility for tax benefits | ⬜ |
| B6 | **File the trademark** in classes **9** (software), **41** (education) and **42** (SaaS) | The startup/MSME fee is lower per class. File as soon as the name is final | ⬜ |
| B7 | **Publish the legal pages** at `[DOMAIN]/privacy`, `/terms`, `/refunds` and `/contact` | Payment gateways require these pages before approving you | ⬜ |
| B8 | **Payment gateway** (e.g. Razorpay) | Needs the company PAN, bank account, website and the legal pages | ⬜ |
| B9 | **Lawyer review** of the Privacy Policy, Terms, parental flow and Shareholders' Agreement | One review with good drafts is quick | ⬜ |

## Phase C: as you grow
| # | Trigger | Step |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Turnover over **₹20 lakh a year** (services) | **GST registration** becomes mandatory; you can register voluntarily earlier. Charge 18% GST on subscriptions once registered |
| C2 | Selling to colleges | A standard **institution agreement**: seats, price, data roles (DPDP fiduciary vs processor), SLA |
| C3 | First employee or intern | Offer letter with an **IP assignment** and confidentiality clause; register under the Delhi Shops & Establishments Act |
| C4 | First outside investor | Term sheet, valuation, and an ESOP pool (unanimous decision, founders' agreement clause 6) |
| C5 | Real user base | **Cyber liability insurance**, and a yearly security review |

## Every year (Private Limited Company)
- **Statutory audit** of accounts (mandatory, even with zero revenue)
- **ROC filings:** financial statements (AOC-4) and annual return (MGT-7/7A)
- **Income tax return** (ITR-6), plus directors' KYC
- **GST returns**, monthly or quarterly once registered

Budget a CA retainer for these. Missing ROC filings means daily late fees.
