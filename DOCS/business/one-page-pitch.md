# Nidanos

**AI that makes itself unnecessary.** Learn to code without a crutch.

**The problem.** Learners watch courses, copy the instructor's code, and ask AI for answers. Then they face a blank editor and can't build anything alone. We measured it on one learner after months of courses: **10/11 at reading code, 0/4 at writing it.** AI assistants make this worse. In a randomised trial with about 1,000 students, those who practised with unrestricted AI help scored **17% lower** once the AI was taken away (Bastani et al., *PNAS* 2025). Only **42.6%** of Indian graduates are rated employable (Mercer-Mettl Graduate Skill Index 2025).

**Our inspiration: the gurukul.** The guru teaches, guides and corrects. When the learning is complete, the shishya goes out into the world and survives alone. Today's apps do the opposite: they're built to keep you coming back, not to let you go.

**The product: AI-Tutor by Nidanos** *(working name, not final)*. An adaptive coding tutor that **withdraws its help as you prove yourself.**
- **Real code, judged for real.** Every submission is compiled and run against hidden tests in a secure sandbox, just as in placement interviews.
- **Honest progress.** Every skill has two scores built only from code you actually ran, one for **reading** code and one for **writing** it, never "80% complete".
- **A tutor that won't hand over answers.** Socratic hints that make you think, never the solution code.
- **Help that fades.** Each skill starts with full support, which drops stage by stage as you solve problems with few hints, until you need none.

**Our edge.** Other platforms optimise for engagement. **We optimise for the day you don't need us.**

**Traction.**
- **Working core, live-tested:** code judging in about 6 seconds, the mastery engine and help-fading are built and verified end to end, with 69 automated tests.
- **Research-backed design:** Bloom's mastery learning, spacing, desirable difficulties.
- **India-ready:** DPDP Act 2023 consent, privacy policy and terms already in place.
- **Next:** a free pilot with 50–100 students.

**Market.** India produces about **1.5 million engineering graduates a year**, and **3.9 lakh** students joined B.Tech Computer Science in 2024-25 alone (AICTE). India's professional online courses market is **$3.9B (2025), growing ~21% a year** (Technavio). **First users:** students preparing Data Structures & Algorithms in C++ for placements, through a freemium subscription.
