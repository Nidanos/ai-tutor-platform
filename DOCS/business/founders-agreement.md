## 1. Purpose
The Founders are building an adaptive programming-learning product (working name `[PRODUCT]`) under the name **Nidanos**, and intend to sell it commercially. This agreement sets out how they own, run and protect it.

## 2. The company
2.1 The Founders will incorporate a **private limited company** named Nidanos (or the closest available name) when they all agree, and no later than before the first paid customer or the first outside investment.
2.2 *Why a private limited company:* shares can vest (clause 4), investors expect it, and it limits each Founder's personal liability.
2.3 Until incorporation, the Founders run the business together on these terms.

## 4. Vesting (protects everyone if someone leaves early)
4.1 Each Founder's shares vest over **4 years** from `[VESTING START DATE]` (suggested: the date work on the project began).
4.2 **1-year cliff:** nothing vests in the first 12 months. At 12 months, **25%** vests, and the rest vests in equal **monthly** parts over the next 36 months.
4.3 After incorporation this is done by **reverse vesting**: each Founder holds all their shares from day one, and the company may buy back the unvested part at **face value** if the Founder leaves.
4.4 **Sale of the company:** if Nidanos is sold and a Founder is removed without cause within 12 months after the sale, all of that Founder's unvested shares vest immediately. This is called "double-trigger" acceleration.

## 5. Leaving: good and bad leavers
| | Good leaver | Bad leaver |
|---|---|---|
| **When** | Death, serious illness or disability, or leaving by mutual written agreement | Fraud, a serious breach of this agreement, gross misconduct, joining or starting a competing business while a Founder, or leaving within the cliff year without agreement |
| **Unvested shares** | Bought back at face value | Bought back at face value |
| **Vested shares** | Kept, or bought by the company at **fair market value**, at the company's option | Bought back at the **lower of face value or fair market value** |

The remaining Founders decide fair market value with an independent valuer if they can't agree on it.

## 6. Decisions
6.1 **Unanimous (all three)** for: issuing or transferring shares; changing ownership or vesting; raising investment or taking loans over ₹1,00,000; selling the company or its main assets; adding a founder; changing this agreement; closing the business.
6.2 **Majority (2 of 3)** for everything else, including product, hiring, pricing and spending under the limits above.
6.3 Each Founder leads day-to-day decisions in their own area (clause 3).

## 7. Intellectual property
7.1 Every Founder **assigns to Nidanos** all rights in everything they create for the product, whether before or after this agreement, and before or after incorporation: code, designs, documents, data, content, names and logos. Before incorporation, it's held jointly in trust for the future company and assigned to it on incorporation.
7.2 No Founder may add third-party code, content or assets to the product unless its licence allows commercial use.
7.3 Each Founder confirms that their contribution doesn't belong to an employer or institution, or that they have a written release (see `college-noc-letter.md`).

## 8. Confidentiality and non-solicitation
8.1 Founders keep business information confidential (code, data, users, plans, finances) during this agreement and for **3 years** after leaving.
8.2 While a Founder, and for **12 months** after leaving, a Founder won't persuade Nidanos's users, customers, team members or partners to leave Nidanos.
8.3 While a Founder, no Founder works on a competing learning product without unanimous consent.

## 9. Time, money and pay
9.1 Each Founder gives the time needed for their role, and tells the others early if that changes (for example during exams).
9.2 No salaries until the Founders unanimously agree, after revenue or funding.
9.3 Reasonable business expenses, agreed in advance by a majority, are recorded and repaid by the company once it has money.
9.4 Any money a Founder puts in is recorded as a **loan**, unless all three agree in writing that it's for equity.

## 10. Disputes
10.1 **Talk first:** the Founders try to settle it among themselves within **30 days**.
10.2 **Mediation:** if that fails, they use a neutral mediator in New Delhi, sharing the cost equally, for up to 30 more days.
10.3 **Arbitration:** if that fails, a sole arbitrator decides under the **Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996**. Seat and venue: New Delhi. Language: English. The decision is final and binding.
10.4 This agreement is governed by the laws of India.

## 11. General
11.1 Changes to this agreement must be in writing and signed by all three Founders.
11.2 If one clause is invalid, the rest still applies.
11.3 After incorporation, the Shareholders' Agreement replaces this one wherever they conflict.
